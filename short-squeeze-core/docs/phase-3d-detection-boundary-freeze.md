# Phase 3D Detection Boundary Freeze

`phase_3d_detection_boundary_policy.v1` permits the first objective discovery timestamp, first eligible completed bar at or after discovery, original-platform surfaced timestamp, or manually reconstructed timestamp with explicit approved evidence. Ties use timestamp and source-artifact ID.

The boundary is serialized and identified before outcome access. Highest later return, earliest point before a known spike, most favorable classification, best interval, and outcome-threshold crossing are prohibited. The runtime explicitly rejects the outcome-aware rule fixture and leaves an unresolved or ambiguous boundary blocked.
