# Phase 3B Rule-Outcome Matrix

The matrix has one row per completed registered case and one column per Phase 3A rule in policy order. Cells retain the textual outcomes `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICTED`, `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`; they are never numerically encoded. Separate columns retain detection, outcome label, classification, provenance, and original-platform status.

Frequency summaries are descriptive counts and exact numerator/denominator rates. Outcome-conditioned and category summaries do not rank rules or candidates. Missingness is explicit for short interest, days to cover, borrow fee/availability, float, relative-volume history, news timestamps, SEC filings, provider scope, conflicts, and insufficient history.

Limitations: the registered sample is small and mixed-provenance; historical coverage is incomplete; original-platform status may be unknown; prevalence is not predictive value; missing short-pressure evidence may dominate; public sources can differ from original providers; detection/outcome policies are provisional and unoptimized; no causal, statistical-validation, P&L, or trading claim is made.
