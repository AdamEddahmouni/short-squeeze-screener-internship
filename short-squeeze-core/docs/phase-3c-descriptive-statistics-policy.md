# Phase 3C Descriptive Statistics Policy

`phase_3c_descriptive_statistics_policy.v1` permits count-first detection, outcome, research-classification, rule-outcome, missingness, data-quality, and confusion-matrix summaries. Rates retain exact numerators, denominators, fractions, Decimal values, and explicit undefined reasons.

The preferred term is **descriptive research-classification rate**. These statistics do not validate predictive performance, estimate squeeze causation, rank rules, optimize thresholds, or produce recommendations. Synthetic TP/FP/TN/FN cells are software truth-table coverage only and are never historical empirical estimates.
