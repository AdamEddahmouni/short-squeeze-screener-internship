# Phase 3D Inclusion and Exclusion Policy

`phase_3d_historical_inclusion_policy.v1` requires a preregistered/active plan, in-range and in-population discovery, recorded provenance, validated artifacts, resolvable identity, a deterministic boundary, objective market evidence, and a constructible Phase 3A request without fabrication. Missing short-pressure evidence may remain unknown or insufficient and does not automatically exclude. A later non-move is never an exclusion reason.

`phase_3d_historical_exclusion_policy.v1` defines the exact `ExclusionCode` vocabulary in the Phase 3D model, including date/population, duplicates, identity, boundary, market data, provenance, artifact integrity, leakage, post-event sourcing, current-as-historical misuse, provider scope, corporate actions, symbol reuse, plan state, fabrication, and manual review. Every exclusion stores the attempt identity, evidence references, limitations, and remediation context; exclusion never deletes the attempt.
