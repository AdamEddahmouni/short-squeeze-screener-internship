## Handoff continuation audit

A follow-up audit against the Phase 3D requirements found one defect. The leakage audit collected the plan, boundary, Phase 3A request, and Phase 3A result freeze timestamps but compared only the result against outcome capture, so three of the four documented ordering controls were unenforced. Each stage is now audited independently. Committed fixtures and anchors were unaffected because their freeze timestamps were already correctly ordered.

The curation lifecycle keeps its documented `RESEARCH_EVALUATED` → `REVIEWED` → `PUBLISHED` terminal chain with `PARTIAL` and `BLOCKED` review states rather than separate `CURATED_COMPLETE` and `CURATED_PARTIAL` names; the states are equivalent and the existing naming is retained to avoid regenerating stable identities.

No remotes were added, and nothing was pushed, merged, rebased, amended, or squashed. Phase 3E was not started.
